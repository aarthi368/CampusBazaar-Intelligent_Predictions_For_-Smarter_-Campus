What Is CampusBazar?

A Smarter Way to Trade on Campus

CampusBazar is a student-only campus marketplace where you can buy, sell, and exchange useful items within your academic community. Unlike generic marketplaces, CampusBazar is designed specifically for the campus environment.

What makes us different? Our ML-powered prediction system analyzes your listing and predicts how quickly it will be claimed — before you even post it. This helps you optimize your listings for maximum visibility and faster trades.
